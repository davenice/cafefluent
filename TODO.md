## Mixed quiz across the enabled modules
- One quiz drawing 5 random questions from each enabled module, so a learner can
  revise everything at once rather than a module at a time.
- Its own card on the home page and its own tickbox on the admin page, like the
  table game. Off until it is ticked.
- Each module's five are spread across its quiz types, and distractors come from
  within the question's own module.
- `sequence-order` is left out: one run of it scores out of eleven, so it cannot
  be one question among twenty.
